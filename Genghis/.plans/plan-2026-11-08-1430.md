### Summary of the Problem

When a battle ends abnormally (server hangs instead of clean shutdown), `OnBattleStoped()` is never called by the CLoD engine. Several `System.Threading.Timer` objects keep firing indefinitely on thread-pool threads:

| Timer | File | Problem |
|-------|------|---------|
| `threadLoadTimer` | `Genghis-Class-ThreadLoad.cs` | `threadLoadTimer_dispose()` exists but is **never called** from `OnBattleStoped()` |
| `tempFlakTimer` | `Genghis.cs` | Disposed in `OnBattleStoped()`, but callback may already be queued |
| `COVER_ListPositionTimer` | `Genghis-Class-CoverMission.cs` | Disposed in `OnBattleStoped()`, but dictionary not cleared |
| Cover display timers | `Genghis-Class-CoverMission.cs` | May not fire `OnBattleStoped()` in failure scenarios |

The log shows TempFlak and cover position display kept running for ~7.5 hours after the server hung.
